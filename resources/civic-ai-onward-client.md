## Key Decisions and Context
- **"Alternative culture" positioning:** Not bro-branding, not a DEI-first pitch. Names the conflict of interest in the dominant vendor narrative (the frontier labs + big consulting have "chosen camps" and are paid to sell tokens / bake in switching costs). Shaina's framing: "we're not saying no to anything; there is another way."
- **Vendor neutrality is the differentiator:** the team takes no cut of tokens, so it is free to tell the whole story. The "stack" framing (model, harness, tools, skills, people, data) shows how orgs stay modular vs. locking into one family.
- **Anti-AI sentiment is a tailwind:** collapsing public attitudes mean demand for a credible alternative voice. Not doomsaying - it is about who captures the value.
- **Civic thesis:** AI creates value; the question is who captures it (platforms vs. local businesses/cities). Big employers get the help and expect to cut jobs (tax-base erosion); SMBs - the lifeblood, and the ones who would actually hire - are unsupported.
- **Foundation-funding strategy:** find a foundation (Ford-with-Maryland model, MacArthur) to fund multi-city work and clear procurement friction, rather than going city-by-city.
